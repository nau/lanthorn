## Token refresh

- The token service MUST issue a new refresh token on every refresh. `[TR-1]`
- The token service MUST revoke the presented refresh token before it returns the new one. `[TR-2]`
- The token service MUST revoke the whole family on reuse of a revoked token. `[TR-3]`
- The token service MUST reject a refresh token older than 30 days. `[token-refresh.max-age]`

I kept the existing `TR-` IDs, because published IDs are frozen. The new rule takes a word ID. Its first level, `token-refresh`, is not `tr`, so you may want to rename it to match your area name.

I did not add an example or a "Why" note, because the source does not settle two points. You may want to decide them and add a boundary example:

- **Start of the age:** the rule does not say if the 30 days run from issuance of the refresh token or from the creation of its family.
- **Boundary:** the rule does not say what happens at exactly 30 days. "Older than" reads as accept, but an implementer could read it the other way.
