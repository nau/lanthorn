## Token refresh

- The token service MUST issue a new refresh token on every refresh. `[TR-1]`
- The token service MUST revoke the presented refresh token before it returns the new one. `[TR-2]`
- The token service MUST revoke the whole family on reuse of a revoked token. `[TR-3]`
- The token service MUST reject a refresh token older than 30 days. `[refresh.max-age]`

I kept `[TR-1]` to `[TR-3]` unchanged, because published IDs are frozen. The new rule takes a word ID. I used `refresh` as the area name, since I don't know the area file's name. Rename it if the area has a different stem.

The rule leaves two points open. I did not guess at them, because that would add requirements you did not give me:

- **Start of the age:** the rule does not say whether the 30 days count from issue of the token or from the first token in its family.
- **Boundary:** the rule does not say what happens at exactly 30 days. Say which reading you want and I will add an example table.
