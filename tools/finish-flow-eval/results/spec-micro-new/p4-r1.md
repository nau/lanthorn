The new rule:

> The auth service MUST reject a refresh token whose age is greater than 30 days. `[auth.refresh-max-age]`
>
> *Example, illustrative.* Refresh token issued at `t0`:
>
> | Server time | Result |
> |---|---|
> | `t0` + 30 days − 1 second | accept |
> | `t0` + 30 days | accept, the age is not greater than 30 days |
> | `t0` + 30 days + 1 second | reject |
>
> **Why:** A stolen refresh token that is never used again would otherwise stay valid forever. The limit bounds how long it is useful.
